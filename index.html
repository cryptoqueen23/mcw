Liberty & Privacy

Your face is not government property.

Technology can make a city safer and more efficient, but public convenience should never become an excuse for permanent, unchecked data collection.

Consent before biometric collection Limits on surveillance retention and sharing Transparent city use of emerging technologies Protection for residents who choose cash
Core belief

Featured civic proposal

Rochester Sovereignty, Biometric Privacy & Cash Access Act

Local rules for a changing digital world.

A municipal framework focused on biometric privacy, responsible data collection, public transparency, and protecting residents from being forced into a cashless public life.

Support the Proposal Read Full Text
Biometric Privacy

Set clear boundaries around facial recognition and other biometric systems.

Cash Access

Preserve practical payment choice for residents who rely on or prefer physical cash.

Government Transparency

Require public accountability when agencies deploy surveillance or data-driven systems.

Local Sovereignty

Keep Rochester residents at the center of decisions affecting their rights and daily lives.

City priorities

Practical reform, neighborhood by neighborhood.

Public Safety

Safe streets without surrendering civil liberty.

Community policing, visible patrols, better lighting, youth prevention, anti-theft measures, and clear accountability.

Jobs & Business

Make Rochester easier to build in.

Reduce unnecessary barriers, support local entrepreneurs, attract employers, and encourage neighborhood-scale investment.

Housing

More ownership. Less red tape.

Modernize zoning and building processes, support affordable development, and help more families move toward homeownership.

Education & Youth

Teach skills that create independence.

Expand vocational pathways, apprenticeships, tutoring, employer partnerships, and civic education for Rochester youth.

Meet Marcus

Rochester isn’t a talking point. It’s home.

Marcus C. Williams is a Rochester civic voice, entrepreneur, business consultant, and community advocate focused on public accountability, financial literacy, neighborhood opportunity, and stronger local institutions.

His public message is built around a simple idea: people do not need more political division. They need leadership that listens, tells the truth, and fights for practical results.
